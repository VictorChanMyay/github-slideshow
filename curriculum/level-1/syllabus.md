# Level 1: Digital Foundations (ages 13–15)

**Duration:** 18 weeks (3 courses × 6 weeks), about 2–3 hours a week (one 60–90 min class plus 1 hour of practice).
**Entry requirement:** None.
**Exit goal:** The learner can use a computer independently, type with confidence and stay safe online.

---

## Course 1.1: Computer Basics (Weeks 1–6)

| Week | Topic | Hands-on activities |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Meet the computer.** Hardware vs. software; input, output, storage, processing; desktop vs. laptop vs. tablet vs. phone | Label the parts of a real computer, then power on and off safely |
| 2 | **Operating systems.** Desktop, taskbar, windows, menus, settings, user accounts | Personalize the desktop, change language and volume, switch between windows |
| 3 | **Files and folders.** Create, name, copy, move, rename, delete, search, recycle bin, file types (.docx, .jpg, .pdf) | Build a folder system for school subjects; find "lost" files in a scavenger hunt |
| 4 | **Apps and software.** Install and uninstall safely, updates, free vs. paid, official sources | Install a safe app and review its permissions |
| 5 | **Devices and storage.** USB drives, cloud storage, printers, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, backups | Back up a folder to the cloud and a USB drive; connect to a printer or Wi-Fi |
| 6 | **Troubleshooting.** Frozen apps, slow computer, restarting, asking for help, basic care | Fix 5 simulated problems. **Final project:** set up and organize a personal workspace |

**Assessment:** Practical checklist (60%), workspace project (30%), participation (10%).

---

## Course 1.2: Typing and Productivity (Weeks 7–12)

| Week | Topic | Hands-on activities |
|---|---|---|
| 7 | **Posture and home row.** Ergonomics, finger placement, ASDF JKL; | Baseline typing test, then 15 min of home-row drills |
| 8 | **Full keyboard.** Top and bottom rows, numbers, shift, punctuation | Typing games and short passages, aiming for 20 wpm |
| 9 | **Word processing basics.** Typing, selecting, copy/paste, undo, fonts, size, alignment, spell check | Write and format a one-page "About Me" |
| 10 | **Formatting documents.** Headings, bullet lists, line spacing, images, page layout, print preview | Create a flyer for a school event |
| 11 | **Keyboard shortcuts and speed.** Ctrl+C/V/X/Z/S/F, Alt+Tab, screenshots, accuracy over speed | Shortcut challenge; typing at 30 wpm with 95% accuracy |
| 12 | **Putting it together.** Proofreading, saving as PDF, sharing a document | **Final project:** a formatted report or CV saved as DOCX and PDF |

**Assessment:** Typing test (30 wpm, 95% accuracy) (40%), final document (40%), shortcut quiz (20%).
**Tools:** Typing.com, Keybr, TypingClub, Google Docs or Word.

---

## Course 1.3: Internet and Online Safety (Weeks 13–18)

| Week | Topic | Hands-on activities |
|---|---|---|
| 13 | **How the internet works.** Websites, browsers, URLs, Wi-Fi, servers (simple view) | Dissect a web address; trace the "journey" of a web page |
| 14 | **Searching well.** Keywords, search operators, tabs, bookmarks, downloads, ads vs. results | Research scavenger hunt with 5 questions |
| 15 | **Judging information.** Spotting fake news, checking sources, reliable vs. unreliable sites, citing sources | Compare 3 sources on one topic and rate their trustworthiness |
| 16 | **Accounts and passwords.** Strong passwords, password managers, 2-step verification, phishing, scams | Build a strong passphrase; spot phishing emails in a mock inbox |
| 17 | **Privacy and digital footprint.** Social media settings, what not to share, location data, permanent posts | Audit privacy settings (on practice accounts); map your own digital footprint |
| 18 | **Respect and wellbeing.** Cyberbullying, what to do if targeted or witnessing, screen-time balance, reporting and blocking, copyright basics | **Final project:** a "stay safe online" guide (poster, slides or short video) for younger students |

**Assessment:** Safety scenario quiz (30%), source-evaluation task (20%), final guide (40%), participation (10%).

---

## Level 1 completion checklist

I can…
- [ ] Create, organize and find files and folders
- [ ] Install an app safely and fix simple problems
- [ ] Type at 30+ wpm with good accuracy
- [ ] Write and format a document and save it as a PDF
- [ ] Search effectively and judge whether a source is reliable
- [ ] Make strong passwords, spot phishing and manage privacy settings
- [ ] Respond to cyberbullying and ask for help

**Next step:** Level 2 (documents, spreadsheets, collaboration).

---

## Teaching notes
- Use short demonstrations (10–15 min), then hands-on practice for the rest of the class.
- If devices are scarce, pair learners and rotate roles (driver and navigator).
- If internet is limited, use offline activities in weeks 1–12 and downloaded materials in weeks 13–18.
- Use practice accounts or simulated social media for the privacy lessons, not learners' personal accounts.
